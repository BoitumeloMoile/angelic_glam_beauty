Booking policy & terms

Please read our booking policy carefully before making an appointment. By booking with Angelic Glam Beauty Bar, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms.

Deposits

A R50 non-refundable deposit is required to secure and confirm your appointment.

The deposit must be paid through our secure online payment gateway at the time of booking. Your appointment will not be confirmed until payment has been successfully received.

The R50 deposit will be deducted from the total cost of your service on the day of your appointment.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Appointments may be rescheduled provided that at least 24 hours' notice is given.

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 24 hours before your appointment, as well as missed appointments, will result in the forfeiture of your deposit.

A new R50 deposit will be required to make another booking.

Late arrivals

Please arrive on time for your appointment.

Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to have their appointment rescheduled, depending on the remaining time available.

If your appointment is rescheduled due to lateness, your deposit will be forfeited, and a new deposit will be required to secure another appointment.

Nail Preparation & Soak offs

Please arrive with clean, bare nails unless you have specifically booked a soak off or removal service.

If you arrive with existing nail product, and a soak off was not included in your booking, an additional soak-off fee will be charged.

Guests & Safety

For safety and security reasons, clients may bring a maximum of one additional person to their appointment.

Payment

The R50 deposit is paid securely through our online payment gateway when making your booking.

The remaining balance must be paid after your service has been completed, together with any applicable additional charges, including soak-offs, nail repairs, extra length, or additional nail art.

No shows

Failure to arrive for your appointment without prior notice will be considered a no show

Your R50 deposit will be forfeited, and a new deposit will be required before making another booking

Privacy

We collect your name, email, and phone number to manage your account and appointments. We don't sell your information to third parties. Contact us if you'd like your data removed.

By booking an appointment, you agree to these terms.
