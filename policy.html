Deposits

A $20 deposit is required to confirm any appointment. This deposit is non-refundable and is applied toward the total cost of your service on the day of your visit.

Cancellations & rescheduling

You may reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours in advance at no additional cost. Cancellations or reschedules made with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments, will forfeit the deposit.

Late arrivals

Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled, and the deposit will not be applied to a new booking.

Payment

Deposits are processed securely through Stripe at the time of booking. The remaining balance is due at checkout after your service.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, and phone number to manage your account and appointments. We don't sell your information to third parties. Contact us if you'd like your data removed.

By booking an appointment, you agree to these terms.
